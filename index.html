Installing

  • Tap the button above on the phone you want to install it on.
  • Allow installs from your browser when Android asks, and continue past the Play Protect note — the app is not distributed through Google Play.
  • If Kindpair is already installed from Google Play, uninstall it first. The Play build is signed with a different key, so this download cannot update over it.
